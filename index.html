The House

Azimuth, since 2010

Founded on the quays of Port Hercules by a captain and a charter broker who had grown tired of brochures that outran the boats. We keep a small fleet we know by hull number — and we decline weeks we cannot staff to our own standard.

Every captain holds an MCA Yachtmaster Ocean or equivalent. Engineers are STCW certified. Safety drills are not a checklist we file; they are run before every charter, with guests invited to watch.

Luxury, for us, is a quiet engine room, a table that is never late, and a crew that disappears until you need them.
